# Evidence provenance and historical measurement failures

This package releases derived instance-level effects and scientific documentation. It does not release raw provider transport/account metadata or every original adjudication record. The historical descriptions below summarize the associated manuscript and Supplement; they do not substitute for raw-response custody. Original source references remain in the author's retained records and private audit crosswalk.

## Public evidence availability

The matrix distinguishes a result that a reader can recompute from the released derived data from a historical or custody claim reported by the author. A resolvable documentation heading provides a reader-facing explanation; it does not independently substantiate the underlying experiment. Likewise, a SHA-256 identifies bytes when the corresponding artifact is available for comparison. A hash alone does not validate scoring, raw-response custody, completion, chronology or scientific correctness.

## Identification and prospective safeguards

The contextual intervention preserves target-relevant information while changing its assigned provenance role. The [identification framework](identification_framework.md) specifies semantic-information equivalence, computational necessity and the independent inferential unit. The [reproducibility protocol](reproducibility_protocol.md) specifies prospective freezing, completion/readability classification, raw custody, deterministic derivation and the first-attempt stopping rule. The [claim boundaries](claim_boundaries.md) distinguish methodological consequences, exploratory findings and confirmatory non-detections.

## Earlier measurement failures

Earlier prompts reprinted parent values, so the designated contextual record was not computationally necessary. Later instruments removed that information from the current prompt and checked prompt-alone closure. A scorer audit also led to prospective retention of completed, readable, nonexact responses as errors in the denominator. An unnecessary stochastic finalizer was removed prospectively because a second model call could change the measurement of the evaluated response. These repairs do not convert earlier results into confirmatory evidence.

## Corrected overall confirmation

A derived-data extraction failure produced an incorrect all-zero ledger. All 336 raw responses were preserved, and the corrected ledger was reconstructed deterministically under the unchanged oracle, scorer and analyzer, without behavioral replay, row replacement or endpoint change.

The canonical corrected derived ledger contains 336 rows and has SHA-256 `8ce6a19f9e62fd29ecef0ff1ec62708690c1e462c0d75b69dfc7ad1c846382c1`. This identifies the author-held corrected derived ledger; it does not imply that the corrected ledger or raw-response custody artifacts are included in this public package. The released 28 instance effects and their exact file hash are bound by [the public provenance manifest](../empirical/corrected_n28_provenance_manifest.json). The [scalar result](../empirical/corrected_n28_result.json) and [recomputation code](../analysis/recompute_n28.py) document the independent-instance analysis.

The corrected result retained 138/168 exact LOCAL responses and 131/168 exact CROSS responses. The overall estimate was +0.04167, with 95% CI [-0.03821,+0.12155] and two-sided p=.29398: a non-detection, not equivalence.

## Exploratory depth pattern

The first-bank stage estimates were S -0.07143, T +0.05357 and U +0.14286. The post-hoc depth slope was +0.10714. These were exploratory findings; the frozen point estimates, available p values and multiplicity distinctions are retained in [the effect-estimate table](../figure_data/fig4_effect_estimates.csv) and [claim boundaries](claim_boundaries.md). The released N=28 overall vector is sufficient for the overall confirmation, but it does not independently reconstruct all historical stage analyses.

## Resource failure and prospective qualification

An earlier depth-confirmation sample stopped after planned execution 335 failed to complete under a 2048 output cap. Its exposed prefix was not promoted to confirmation, resumed or replayed. A fresh LOCAL-only, non-confirmatory resource qualification at 8192 completed 32/32 responses with zero integrity failures before a new confirmatory sample was exposed. These historical events remain distinct from the fresh N=124 result.

## Fresh prospective confirmation

The fresh confirmation used 124 independent instances and the prespecified primary contrast `L=(D_U-D_S)/2`. All 1,488 planned responses completed, with no retry, replay, replacement or imputation. The primary estimate was +0.016129, with 95% CI [-0.035417,+0.067675], t(123)=0.6194 and p=.536815. The positive depth contrast was not confirmed, and no stage-specific confirmatory claim followed.

The [instance effects](../empirical/fresh_n124_instance_effects.csv), [frozen scalar result](../empirical/fresh_n124_result.json), [data dictionary](../empirical/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) and [recomputation code](../analysis/recompute_n124.py) provide the public derivation. Historical integrity failures and exploratory findings are preserved as scientific evidence, rather than accumulated as positive confirmation.
